# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project exists to make AI safer for everyone. That purpose shapes how we engage with each other.

We are committed to a community where researchers, engineers, safety practitioners, and interested humans from anywhere in the world can participate without harassment, bad faith, or gatekeeping.

---

## Standards

**We expect:**

- Honest engagement — say what you actually think, back it up with reasoning
- Good faith — assume others are trying to help, not to attack
- Intellectual rigor — proposals and challenges should be reasoned, not emotional
- Openness to being wrong — the taxonomy itself might be wrong; so might any of us
- Defense-first orientation — contributions must serve safety, not harm

**We will not tolerate:**

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Contributions designed to operationalize harm (this is a defense project)
- Bad-faith challenges designed to undermine the project rather than improve it
- Misrepresentation of others' work or arguments
- Using this project's infrastructure to spread misinformation about AI capabilities or safety

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public representation of this project.

---

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this standard, contact ryangat@lmlsystemlayer.com. All reports will be reviewed seriously and handled with discretion.

Consequences for violations range from a warning to permanent removal from the project, depending on severity and pattern.

---

## The Bigger Picture

AI is developing faster than humans can collectively reason about its risks. This project is one attempt to slow that gap — to give the global AI community shared vocabulary and shared tools for finding and fixing failures before they cause harm.

That goal is worth protecting. Please help protect it.
